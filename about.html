About AfriLearn AI

Exam prep for students, anywhere

AfriLearn AI is a free study companion built for students preparing for exams — from WASSCE and BECE to SAT, A-Levels, IB, GCSE, and university coursework. Generate practice quizzes on any topic in seconds, or chat with an AI tutor that explains concepts the way you'd want a patient study partner to.

It was built by a student, for students, because the best study tools should be accessible everywhere — not just in well-resourced schools.

How it works

Pick your exam or course level, type in a topic, and AfriLearn AI generates multiple-choice practice questions with explanations — up to 100 at a time. Prefer to talk it through? Switch to the Chat Tutor and ask anything from your syllabus.

Free, with an optional Pro upgrade

The core app is free to use, supported by ads. A one-time Pro upgrade removes ads entirely for those who'd rather study without interruptions.
